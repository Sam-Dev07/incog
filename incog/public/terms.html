1. What incog is

incog lets you create a personal link. Anyone with the link can send you an anonymous message, and only you can read the messages sent to you. By creating an account or sending a message you agree to these terms.

2. Who can use it

You must be at least 13 years old. If you are under 18, you need permission from a parent or guardian.

3. Your account

  • You sign in with Google. You are responsible for your account.
  • Your username is public, because it is part of your link, and it cannot be changed once claimed.
  • Do not impersonate other people or pick a username meant to deceive or offend.

4. What you must not send or do

  • Harassment, bullying, threats or encouragement of violence or self-harm.
  • Hate speech, slurs, or attacks on people for who they are.
  • Sexual or explicit content, or anything involving minors in a sexual way.
  • Sharing private information about someone (doxxing), blackmail, or scams.
  • Spam, illegal content, or trying to break, overload or bypass the service (including its limits and bans).
  • False reports. Reporting a message that does not break these rules is itself a violation.

5. Anonymity and what we store

  • The person who receives a message is never shown who sent it.
  • We store the message text, the receiver's username and a timestamp.
  • To limit spam and abuse, we store a one-way hash of the sender's network address. It is not the address itself, and only the site admin can see it. It is used for the send limit (one message per network every 3 hours) and for bans.
  • For account holders we store your Google account email (through Firebase Authentication), your username, and a hash of the network you last logged in from.
  • Anonymous does not mean untraceable. We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm.

6. Reports, warnings and bans

  • You can report any message you receive. Reports and the reported message go to the admin.
  • The admin may ban a sender's network or account, or warn a user. Warnings are shown the next time you open incog.
  • Networks are sometimes shared (schools, workplaces, mobile carriers), so a ban may affect other people on the same network. Contact us if you think you were blocked by mistake.
  • Moderation decisions are made at the admin's discretion.

incog uses Google Firebase (login and database), Render (hosting), and third-party services to look up network addresses. Their own terms and privacy policies apply to their parts of the service.
